Termite Control Solutions

Three termite methods under one complete service

Different termite problems need different treatment methods. KK Pest Control provides baiting systems, chemical soil treatment, and corrective soil treatment depending on the property condition, infestation level, and protection goal.

Termite Baiting System For colony elimination and long-term monitoring.
Chemical Soil Treatment For soil barrier protection around or under a property.
Corrective Soil Treatment For active termite infestations in existing buildings.
Termite Control Method
🐜

Termite Baiting System

Advanced termite baiting that targets the colony at the source, helps prevent structural damage, and protects your property long term.

What We Do:

  • Termite activity inspection and risk assessment
  • Bait installation to eliminate colonies at the source
  • Low-impact, discreet system suitable for homes and businesses
  • Ongoing monitoring for long-term protection

Best for homes, shoplots, offices, and commercial buildings that need discreet long-term termite protection.

Chemical Soil Treatment

Chemical soil treatment creates a protective treated barrier around or under the property to stop termites from entering and reduce the risk of future infestation.

What We Do:

  • Inspect the property perimeter and soil treatment zones
  • Apply approved chemicals into targeted soil areas
  • Create a treated barrier for deep, long-lasting protection
  • Recommend prevention steps for future termite risk

Best for construction, renovation, perimeter protection, and properties that need a strong soil barrier.

Termite Control Method
🎯

Corrective Soil Treatment

Corrective soil treatment is used for active termite problems in existing buildings, with targeted soil injection to treat termite entry zones and protect against further damage.

What We Do:

  • Identify active termite zones and likely entry points
  • Inject treatment directly into targeted soil areas
  • Treat hidden pathways near walls, doors, and structural edges
  • Provide follow-up advice for long-term termite control

Best for existing homes, shoplots, offices, and buildings with active termite activity or visible signs of termite entry.

What We Do:

  • Residual spray for cracks and hiding spots
  • ULV fogging for heavier infestations
  • Sanitation and prevention guidance

Rodent Treatment

Professional rat and mouse control using traps, bait stations, entry-point checks, and practical prevention to keep rodents out.

What We Do:

  • Inspect entry points, nesting areas, and rodent trails
  • Place strategic traps and secured bait stations
  • Recommend sealing for gaps, drains, pipes, and wall openings
  • Monitor activity until the rodent problem is reduced

Recommended for homes, restaurants, kitchens, storerooms, warehouses, and shoplots.

  • Breeding-source inspection and prevention advice
  • Regular fogging packages for stronger protection

Bed Bug Treatment

Professional bed bug treatment for bedrooms, mattresses, sofas, furniture, hotels, hostels, and rental units.

  • Prevention recommendations to reduce re-infestation risk

Best for homes, hotels, staff quarters, hostels, Airbnb units, and rental rooms.
